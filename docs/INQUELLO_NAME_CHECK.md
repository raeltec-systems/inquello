# Inquello — deeper name, trademark and handle screen

**Checked:** October 26, 2026  
**Priority:** Zambia. South Africa and Kenya are the initial additional African markets; regional routes were reviewed for coverage, not cleared country by country.  
**Conclusion:** retain Inquello as the preferred working candidate, but **do not treat it as trademark-cleared or finally selected**. Public handles look promising. Similar INKWELL software names and filings need a targeted local review.

## 1. What changed since the first shortlist

No exact Inquello auditor/software brand surfaced in the bounded public searches. The deeper pass did identify a substantial phonetic similarity question: Inquello, pronounced “in-KWELL-oh,” resembles **Inkwell**, which is already used for AI software.

A returned official Canadian record identifies **INKWELL application 2402599**, filed May 31, 2025 by Inkwell Technology Studios, Inc. Its goods/services include downloadable AI software and AI SaaS in classes 9 and 42, plus class 38 services. The returned PDF shows a pending examination status, but its cached version includes a September 4, 2026 response deadline; the later disposition was not verified. It also references US priority filings **98884430, 98884433 and 98884438** dated December 4, 2024. This is evidence of a similar mark and overlapping subject matter, **not proof of a Zambian right or an infringement conclusion**. [Official CIPO record](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/pdf/2402599?lang=eng)

[Inkwell Software](https://www.inkwell.software/) separately markets an AI writing environment with agents, document editing, version control and reviewable changes. Its professional audience differs, but the working-environment concept is close enough to merit scrutiny. No relationship between that site and the Canadian applicant was established.

Other names for a professional search include Apple's [Inkwell application-program brand](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/trademark/appletmlist.html), [Inquiro data-analysis software](https://www.inquiro.dev/) and Oracle's historical [InQuira knowledge-software acquisition](https://www.oracle.com/corporate/pressrelease/oracle-buys-inquira-072811.html). These are search leads, not findings that all are legally conflicting or currently registered in the chosen African markets.

## 5. Concrete next step before settling the name

Obtain a **Zambia exact-and-similarity search**, with specific attention to Inquello versus INKWELL and overlapping software services. A search that checks only exact INQUELLO is insufficient for the issue found here.

PACRA's live [forms and fees page](https://www.pacra.org.zm/fees-and-forms?q=search), inspected directly in the browser, currently lists **TM Form 29**, with **K55.60 local / K466.80 foreign** fees and a required lodgement schedule. It describes this as a search without the Registrar's advice. These are published amounts, not a confirmed total for this proposed multi-name/multi-class request; fee basis and current submission method remain to be confirmed. A TM29 result alone is not an opinion on confusing similarity or freedom to use.

The starting goods/services are **class 9** for downloadable applications and **class 42** for hosted software/SaaS. Exact specifications need to match the actual offering. Additional audit/advisory/training services should only be included if actually planned under the brand; a tool used by auditors is not automatically an audit firm. [WIPO class 9](https://nclpub.wipo.int/esen/pdf-download.pdf?classNumber=9&dateInForce=20260101&lang=en&tab=&viewMode=flat), [WIPO class 42](https://nclpub.wipo.int/esen/pdf-download.pdf?classNumber=42&dateInForce=20260101&lang=en&tab=&viewMode=flat)

A prepared [search brief](research/2026-10-26-inquello-clearance/TRADEMARK_SEARCH_BRIEF.md) sets out the candidate, territories, variants, product scope and questions. It has not been sent or submitted. The official search results should then be reviewed by a qualified local trademark professional before committing to public branding.
