# Inquello — logo and wordmark exploration 01

Date: October 26, 2026

Create an identity for the auditor workspace shown in the user's Claude Design prototype. The product supports inquiry, analysis, practical work and review across audit disciplines. Its light UI uses a pale sidebar, dark typography, subdued blue selection and green/amber workflow states.

## Directions

- **A — Open Inquiry:** a softly squared, open q with an integrated vertical descender; a humanist sans-serif wordmark.
- **B — Evidence Index:** staggered solid strokes and precise negative space; a contemporary sturdy serif wordmark.
- **C — Working Together:** paired uprights with a connecting shoulder inspired by the double-l; a warmer sans-serif wordmark.

## Evaluation

Judge the symbol as a silhouette, alongside the exact name **Inquello**, in a small sidebar masthead and as a positive/reversed app icon. The symbol should stand without colour; colour can support rather than define it. Preserve the legibility of I, q and the two l letters.

Prefer useful distinction and clear proportions over a complicated story. Avoid literal certification/security icons, audit ticks, search magnifiers, generic AI stars and close copies of existing brands.

These generated boards are raster design concepts. Small-size labels and sample UI applications are illustrative; production optical sizing and vector construction have not yet been validated. Final master assets follow selection and refinement of a direction.
