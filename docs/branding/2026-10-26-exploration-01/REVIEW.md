# Inquello — first visual review

Date: October 26, 2026

These three generated concept boards were visually reviewed against the supplied prototype. They are exploration assets, not approved production artwork.

## Recommendation

Develop **A — Open Inquiry** first. It has the clearest connection to the name, a compact silhouette and a geometric wordmark that fits the restrained application. Refine the symbol before committing: the open bowl can also read as an abstract a, and its weight dominates the wordmark in the large lockup.

## Directions shown

| Direction | What works in the generated board | What to refine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [A — Open Inquiry](A-open-inquiry.png) | Open, softly squared q; coherent geometric sans-serif lettering; convincing black and reversed silhouettes. The small masthead feels at home in the product. | Balance symbol height and weight with the name; preserve the opening at small sizes; check whether people read q or a. |
| [B — Evidence Index](B-evidence-index.png) | Staggered blocks paired with a sturdy serif wordmark give an editorial, established character. The serif is a useful alternative to the other two directions. | The mark can read as a generic stack or chart. Explore a more distinctive relationship between the blocks without adding detail. |
| [C — Working Together](C-working-together.png) | Warm geometric lettering; custom upper terminals on the double-l; simple positive and reversed forms. | The symbol reads more readily as h than as paired l letters. The narrow shoulder connection needs testing and simplification for small icons. |

All three boards spell **Inquello** correctly and illustrate light/dark use. The blue variants are exploratory. Keep the core identity monochrome; reserve green and amber for workflow statuses in the application.

## Next refinement after selection

1. Refine one selected symbol and wordmark together, including spacing and relative scale.
2. Produce clean vector masters with a documented, usable typography source or custom lettering; generated lettering is not a specified font.
3. Inspect actual 16, 24 and 32 pixel icons and the real sidebar placement. The boards' small examples are illustrative, not completed optical-size tests.
4. Export symbol-only, horizontal lockup, monochrome/reversed and transparent assets.

The boards contain slight raster shading despite the flat-colour brief; production artwork should use solid fills. Neither name clearance nor logo similarity clearance is established by this visual exploration.
