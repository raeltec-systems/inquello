## Recommendation

**Inquello** is the strongest standalone product brand from this research. **Auditstride** is the clearest choice if the name itself must communicate the audit category. **Scopekind** is the strongest straightforward compound with an exact .com available.

The intended brand is a powerful working environment for auditors across internal audit, external audit, advisory and IT/IS work. It should accommodate V1 assistance in existing tools and external record systems, then V2 native audit records. It should suggest inquiry, useful work and practitioner control without promising automatic correctness or limiting the product to compliance, evidence storage or one methodology.

Rankings below are editorial judgments, not measured customer preference or validated marketing performance.

## What “available” means here

1. **Domain:** the listed available domains were reported registrable by a live registrar lookup at the stated time.
2. **Brand use:** public-web searches screened exact names and selected close variants. No indexing system covers all uses.
3. **Trademark/company name:** formal jurisdiction-specific clearance is **not completed**. No launch jurisdiction was specified. Reading the [USPTO search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search) and [WIPO Global Brand Database information](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) does not constitute running or clearing a trademark search.
4. **Social handles:** comprehensive availability not checked or reserved. Scopekind already has a visible Pinterest handle.
5. **Marketing:** recall, pronunciation and multilingual interpretation have not been tested with users.

A final selection should be narrowed to two names, then checked against similar names and relevant software/services in the intended launch markets, with a short spoken-name/spelling test among auditors. This remaining check does not prevent choosing a preferred creative direction now.
