# Inquello — proposed trademark-search brief

**Prepared:** October 26, 2026  
**Status:** ready for review; not sent, lodged or paid. Applicant details and precise launch countries remain to be supplied.

## Candidate and scope

Proposed word mark: **INQUELLO**. Intended English pronunciation: **in-KWELL-oh**.

Product: an AI-assisted workspace for auditors, supplied as downloadable software and/or hosted software. It assists with evidence analysis, data processing, document/spreadsheet/presentation preparation, review, task coordination and automation. V1 works alongside existing files and record systems; V2 may supply native audit records. The intended offering is software; provision of regulated audit opinions or professional audit services should not be assumed.

Priority territory: **Zambia**. Initial additional territories for scope/fee confirmation: **South Africa and Kenya**. Other African countries should be specified, not treated as automatically covered.

## Requested work

1. Search exact INQUELLO across relevant pending and registered records, including local records and applicable international designations.
2. Search phonetic and visual variants: INQUELO, INKWELLO, INQUELLA, ENQUELLO, INKELLO, INKWELL and INK WELL. Include a reasonable professional similarity search beyond this list.
3. Start related goods/services review with Nice classes 9 and 42; consider other classes and related commercial uses where they may affect the assessment. Confirm the proposed filing specification rather than treating class numbers as a complete description.
4. Give record numbers, proprietors, territories/designations, filing/priority dates, full relevant goods/services, current status and available extracts for potentially relevant results.
5. Distinguish a registry search report from an opinion on registrability, confusing similarity and use of the brand. Identify any further common-law or company-name search needed.

## Specific lead requiring investigation

Canadian INKWELL application **2402599**, with overlapping software goods/services, references US priority serials **98884430, 98884433, 98884438**. Verify current status and whether corresponding rights/designations or relevant use exist in the target African markets. Do not assume Canadian or US rights automatically apply in Zambia.

Confirm applicant name, address and local/foreign status; countries; exact goods/services; search breadth; total fee and payment method; delivery time; current lodgement channel.
