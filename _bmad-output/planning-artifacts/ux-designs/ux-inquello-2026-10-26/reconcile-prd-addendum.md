## Source, service, authority and evidence boundaries

- **Reuse is not proven service availability.** The companion's pinned Core/CLI, app-server and SDK candidates are not promoted into a chosen runtime, reusable desktop or complete hosted scheduler/connector. EXPERIENCE.Foundation and DESIGN.Brand & Style call the imported React/CSS kit an implementation reference, not a framework decision. S07, SP1/SP5 and OQ-6/OQ-9 retain route/compatibility/eligibility checks. The companion retains exact pinned-release and vendor-statement caveats; the UX makes no parity or differentiation claim.
- **Processing concerns remain separate.** Effective route/identity/capability are inspectable in S07/SP1/SP6, and OQ-9 retains actual processing/service eligibility. The governing companion's separation of execution, inference, service traffic, retained files and logs remains unaltered; a provider choice is not presented as resolving these questions.
- **Mechanism does not establish authority or outcome.** Permission to execute, proposed/applied local edit, human decision, Professional Approval and attempted/confirmed/uncertain Delivery are expressly separate in EXPERIENCE.Authority and Delivery. Audit-record writeback does not authorise ledger/HR/source-transaction changes. Source Keep, Stop, Excel and message callbacks are specifically excluded as proof.
- **Method and memory stay scoped.** UJ-7/SP2/SP5 retain guidance/version/dependency, evidence-instruction separation, memory contribution/use and hook-failure visibility. No generic workflow engine, reviewer independence, global rule promotion or policy enforcement by prose is selected.
- **Saved-file and live context differ.** Responsive & Platform and SP6 identify saved file versus supported live/unsaved state; unavailable context is not simulated. Remote version/retry/identity/verification semantics remain required by Authority and Delivery and the linked handoff.
- **Design selection is a later explicit input.** The newly user-selected package supersedes earlier exploratory Open Inquiry material for this UX run. Both spines say so; name clearance, final layouts, accessibility validation and product open choices remain unresolved. This is a documented design-input update, not an accidental promotion of the companion's earlier exploratory branding.
