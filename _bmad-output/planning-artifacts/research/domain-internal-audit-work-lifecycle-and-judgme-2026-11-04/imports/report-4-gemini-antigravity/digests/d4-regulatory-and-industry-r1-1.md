## Executive Regulatory Verification Notes & Corrections
Before presenting the individual claims, three critical regulatory status verifications from 2024–2026 emerged from the search evidence:
1. **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D vs. Appendix B (US OCC):** The OCC *Guidelines Establishing Heightened Standards for Certain Large Insured National Banks, Insured Federal Savings Associations, and Insured Federal Branches* are codified at **12 CFR Part 30, Appendix D** (published September 2014, effective November 10, 2014; asset threshold amendments proposed in 2025/2026), whereas Appendix B covers *Interagency Guidelines Establishing Information Security Standards*.
2. **Chartered IIA UK Financial Services Code Unification (Effective January 2025):** In September 2024 (effective **January 2025**, synchronized with the 2024 IIA Global Internal Audit Standards), the Chartered IIA UK replaced its separate *Financial Services Code of Practice* with a single, **unified *Internal Audit Code of Practice*** (37 principles across 9 areas) covering financial services, private, and third sectors in the UK and Ireland, while retaining financial-sector-specific provisions for capital, liquidity, and customer/conduct risk.
3. **SR 11-7 / OCC Bulletin 2011-12 Update (April 2026):** While SR 11-7 / OCC Bulletin 2011-12 (*Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management*, April 4, 2011) established the three-pillar model validation baseline, the federal banking agencies issued **SR 26-2 / OCC Bulletin 2026-13** in April 2026 to tailor and update model risk management expectations while preserving the core conceptual soundness, ongoing monitoring, and outcomes analysis validation pillars.
