## Customer, problem and positioning
- Confirmed primary user: an internal auditor conducting engagements, with supervisors/managers reviewing. The CAE is a tentative buyer hypothesis. Do not invent an industry, team size, customer interview or named pilot.
- Problem: strategy, objectives, risks, prior findings, actions and evidence are scattered across systems, documents and people; teams repeatedly find, reconcile, analyse and document context across planning, execution, reporting and follow-up.
- Business strategy and objectives are themselves information that must be reconstructed and reused, not merely labels linked to completed work.
- Benefit order: more coverage of significant risks first; quality and business relevance of conclusions second; slower delivery, rework and other consequences follow without a finer agreed ranking. No quantified savings or performance target is approved.
- Approved solution: connected, source-backed organisational understanding plus an agent that actively performs audit work alongside the team, retaining evidence/reasoning across stages while auditors retain direction, judgment and approval.
- Cross-industry and flexible methodology are intended. Use relevant standards as a sensible V1 foundation, distinguish them from organisation-specific practices and external obligations, and learn through hands-on iteration rather than demanding exhaustive practitioner research before useful work.
- Approved headline: “Inquello helps internal audit teams cover more significant risks with an AI agent that understands their organization and works alongside them.”
- Founder fit: Israel's practising-auditor and business-software experience. A dependable, pleasant audit experience is an advantage to earn with real teams, not proven exclusivity or a durable moat.

## Experience and requirement signals
- One conversational workspace directs delegated work. Auditors supply files or permit sources, inspect outputs, question and revise, steer ongoing execution and approve consequential actions.
- The agent should retrieve/question sourced knowledge, perform supported procedures and data analysis, prepare workpapers, and surface exceptions, gaps and evidence. Preserve source, calculation, change and decision inspectability across review and reuse.
- Setup combines guided screens/settings for organisation, roles/permissions, connections and methodology with conversational help for certain preferences and supplied knowledge. Do not put every setting in chat.
- Bring in available strategy/objectives, structure/processes/policies, audit universe/risk picture and useful past work. Define what is required for the first engagement versus what can be added later.
- Approved Getting Started ends: “If something needs a connection or a setting, Inquello points you there.” Preserve that sentence. The explanatory “conversation-forward” discussion was not approved marketing copy.
- Agent access is permission-bounded; admin status must not expose another auditor's private work. External AI processing must be disclosed, allowed connections revocable, and external reporting/evidence sharing separately authorised. These are promises to design and verify.
- Final audit conclusions, formal sign-offs and report issuance remain with people. The agent may execute delegated work within granted authority and must ask when more input or permission is needed.
- Missing information, uncertainty, errors and failed scheduled work remain visible. Preserve completed work, explain known interruption causes, obtain missing inputs/permissions, and allow review, revision or resumption.
- Recurring procedures are delegated, not eliminated. Knowledge accumulated through engagements should support later work without silently propagating stale or unsupported assumptions.
- Portability is an approved promise: usable exported audit work/supporting records outside Inquello, controlled by the organisation. Formats and preservation of evidence–finding–workpaper links remain undecided.
- Pricing transparency is approved; no subscription model, amount, included AI allowance or fixed provider arrangement is approved.
- The leader quote has no named attribution. The approved future-customer quote is explicitly hypothetical. No launch date, location, availability or achieved customer result exists.

## Approved V1 scope
- One organisation and one internal audit team, with an auditor and manager plus admin responsibilities, permissions and a basic methodology. This is an end-to-end journey, not only a technical proof.
- Set up organisation/workspace and bring in available strategy, objectives, audit universe and past work.
- Coverage planning: use organisational context, risks, past findings and outstanding actions to help the manager assess priorities and build a reviewable/changeable audit plan.
- Engagement planning: define objectives, scope, risks, controls and work programme, with agent preparation and team review.
- Audit performance: evidence intake, conversational direction, supported procedures, data analysis, workpapers, exceptions, gaps and inspectable evidence.
- Review/reporting: manager review and change requests, supported findings/report, human formal conclusions and sign-off.
- Follow-up: agreed actions, owners, due dates and progress; unresolved items inform later planning. Include a supported recurring check where data/permissions exist, with visible results and failures.
- The workspace/conversation connects all stages. Auditors should not operate the execution machinery to obtain dependable work.
- Outside V1: external-audit-specific standards/workflows; multiple client organisations/switching for audit firms; large-team reporting hierarchies/resource scheduling/capacity management; broad integration marketplace or every customer's connector; automatic migration of all historical systems; autonomous report issuance or replacement of professional judgment.
- Practical file intake, pilot-required connections and useful history remain in V1. Exact supported procedures/connections, recurrence limits, import formats and assistance must be defined, not assumed unlimited.
- The approved journey does not choose the storage or record-authority architecture. Reconcile older V1 existing-authoritative-system/V2 native-record assumptions explicitly; neither assume full replacement nor narrow the approved journey.

## Hardest challenge and proposed first proof
- Approved working challenge: “Delivering rigorous, long-running audit work at scale through a light conversational experience, with verifiable results that auditors can confidently review without specifying every step or repeating the work themselves.”
- A human cannot recheck millions of records to establish faithful execution. Verification of what ran and what it covered is distinct from professional judgment about whether the chosen procedure was appropriate.
- Coverage, exclusions, failures and interruption recovery must be demonstrable without silent omissions or duplicates. Evidence honesty and knowledge integrity remain supporting challenges.
- Detailed first-proof proposals, not selected architecture/benchmark: define population/scope/exclusions; reconcile completed/excluded/failed/unfinished work; demonstrate safe recovery; expose method/evidence/exceptions/conclusions; distinguish complete/partial/failed honestly; use suitable processing tools behind a simple experience.
- Spot checks alone do not establish full population coverage. No benchmark, dataset, implementation design or detailed pass threshold is approved.
- Earlier technical context: hybrid local execution with shared identity/permissions/collaboration does not imply local inference. Existing plans favour working artifacts and taught methods through instructions/examples/review/refinement; confirm architecture choices during PRD work.

## Approved readiness sequence
- Before real engagement use: demonstrate dependable execution/recovery for supported tasks, verify access controls and audit-information handling, make work/evidence/failures/human review visible, and agree authorised data/use with the pilot organisation.
- Those are real-use blockers, not a demand to support all possible procedures or finish all research before development.
- While building: identify the pilot engagement and sources, specify procedures/connections, establish the baseline, measure costs and decide export formats. Resolve each before the relevant capability is relied on.
- After initial learning: refine pricing and broaden integrations/workflows based on pilot evidence. This does not defer the already-approved full V1 journey or supported recurring check.
- Before asking someone to buy: understand costs and agree the commercial arrangement. The long-term model can still evolve.
- These gates are planning decisions, not current permission to process customer data, conduct outreach, deploy or implement.

## Pilot, adoption and measurement
- Approved adoption risk has two sides: plausible output that fails to become reliable/useful/reviewable audit work, or technically rigorous work that is too cumbersome and sends auditors back to existing tools.
- Test both together on real, appropriately authorised audit work: can auditors direct naturally, trust/review results and actually use them? Observe what is accepted or redone, and refine from behaviour.
- Approved uncomfortable assumption: auditors will incorporate agent work in real engagements, while budget and data-access decision makers permit and pay for use. A pleasant test-data pilot does not prove this.
- Discuss procurement, security and willingness to pay with relevant decision makers under separate outreach/data permissions. V1 development can proceed in parallel; obtain evidence before claiming a proven business case.
- Pilot measures: risk/population coverage, traceable/challengeable/usable quality and significant error detection, comfortable direction/review without repeating every step, and capacity for judgment/business-relevant work.
- Establish baseline and comparisons on actual tasks with the pilot team. No numerical target, percentage improvement, selected protocol or demonstrated result exists.
- Prior success phrase “accepted work with less total effort” includes preparation, review, correction, retries and delivery; do not measure only generation speed.
