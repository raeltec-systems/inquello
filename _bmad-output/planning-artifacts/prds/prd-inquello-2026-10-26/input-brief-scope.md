# Inquello PRD input — brief scope and scenarios

This extract supports PRD creation; it does not replace the source inventory or add accepted product decisions. Sources: [revised brief](../../../../docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF_V0_1.md), [definition addendum](../../../../docs/addendum.md), and [scope traceability](../../../../docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF_TRACEABILITY.md). The user has now confirmed the internal product/design audience and fast path; the brief's earlier audience assumption is superseded by that conversation decision. P01–P22 and J1–J8 are proposed product definitions, not implemented features or observed user stories.

## Accepted direction and boundaries

Sources: addendum sections 1–6, especially lines 17–50 and 103–114.

- **Inquello** is the working name. The foundation is essentially a Codex fork, preserving its broad capabilities and conversational working experience while adapting domain concepts, tools, outputs and review to auditors.
- Serve internal audit, external audit, advisory and IT/IS audit across industries and methodologies. First validation is with one organisation's audit team; that does not fix the permanent market boundary. Neither a formal engagement nor a universal control-testing model is mandatory.
- **V1 targets the full researched Codex mirror plus required audit adaptations, using external authoritative audit systems and supported working files.** Continuous assurance, scheduled/unattended work, technical capabilities and smaller convenience features remain in this target. Full target scope is distinct from the first shipping increment; any deferral is explicit unmet scope, not removal or demonstrated parity.
- **V2 adds native authoritative audit records and lifecycle:** identity, relationships, attribution, reviewed versions, amendments, retention/hold, recovery and export. Mode choice is at organisation/workspace level; precise policy precedence remains open. Native records are not a V1 prerequisite. Operational ledgers/HR systems remain separate; audit-record writeback grants no authority over source transactions.
- Auditors review artifacts inside Inquello or continue on the **same supported saved files** in Excel, Word, PowerPoint and other suitable editors. Saved-file work, live unsaved state/selections and remote platform records are separate capabilities; the baseline does not require an API to every editor or a proprietary conversion.
- Remote record changes are deliberate, reviewable, authorised and verified; import/export remains a fallback. Permission to execute, verification, professional approval, attempted delivery and confirmed delivery remain distinct.
- Default autonomy is a scoped user-started task. Preauthorised unattended capabilities remain available within policy. Methods begin with existing instruction, skill, tool, agent, configuration and memory mechanisms, without selecting a general workflow builder.
- Actual organisational data policy governs processing. No exclusive provider or deployment topology is selected. Amazon Bedrock and Azure Foundry/Azure OpenAI remain explicit routes alongside other researched options.

## People, form factor and work model

Sources: addendum sections 3–5 and P02–P03. Roles are responsibilities, not a fixed permission hierarchy; one person can hold several where policy allows. Preparers investigate and produce outputs; human reviewers challenge evidence/calculations/conclusions and assess revisions; leads scope/coordinate/monitor; technical specialists query data and inspect systems/configuration/code; administrators/method owners govern access and reusable guidance. Guest access and exact publishing/review rights remain open.

The intended form factor spans desktop, CLI/session controls, application-adjacent assistance, mobile/remote steering and optional physical shortcuts. Approved local, isolated and remote execution are distinct from inference/processing location. Specific supported clients and deployment remain design decisions.

The work model comprises organisation/workspace; optional assignment/engagement; task/conversation; source/evidence with origin/version/scope/limits; method/skill/procedure; analysis/test with actual coverage and derivations; artifact/workpaper; observation/finding/recommendation; human review/decision; response/action/retest. These are product concepts, not a selected schema. Advisory options and external-audit assertions/materiality/substantive work must remain possible without mandatory control objects.

## Existing source scenarios

Source: addendum section 8, lines 167–182. All eight are **proposed V1 scenarios**, not observed narratives. External audit-record authority remains in force; V2 can later carry them through native records.

| Journey | Scenario and evidence of success |
| --- | --- |
| J1 | Ask an ad hoc question against existing work; steer analysis and receive a sourced response/artifact with uncertainty, without mandatory engagement setup. |
| J2 | Use the team's method from scope/procedures through evidence, execution, review and reporting; each relied-on result links to scope/evidence/review, without mandatory controls. |
| J3 | Reconcile, inspect, edit the same workbook in Excel, then continue; supported formulas/references/comments survive and changed inputs cause recomputation or stale marking. |
| J4 | Draft/review/revise a Word workpaper and approve through the designated authority; evidence/review history survives and text acceptance is distinct from conclusion approval. |
| J5 | Delegate access/configuration/code-security review, inspect progress and reconcile findings; actual population/coverage, disagreements, gaps and source analysis remain visible. |
| J6 | Research and compare advisory options in a paper/presentation; distinguish facts/assumptions/recommendations and support or label estimates without forcing a deficiency workflow. |
| J7 | Teach from a procedure/example, refine, reuse on another assignment; method/version/parameters remain visible and client evidence does not silently become shared methodology/memory. |
| J8 | A configured schedule/business event starts a bounded procedure, followed by review/delivery and later retest; preserve trigger/coverage/exceptions/delivery/closure authority. Completed runs do not establish assurance or remediation. |

The interest-rate-change example is illustrative; new extracts, policy changes, workpaper submission and due remediation can also be triggers. Each requires a supported event source and bounded mandate; do not imply universal business-event observation.

## Traceability and unresolved choices

The traceability map covers **79 C entries: 76 capability groups plus three composition lenses; 20 E behaviors; 270 detailed subfeature mappings; 93 proposed enhancements**. Existing detailed cards remain scope inputs. Group mapping is not individual-subfeature validation, source eligibility, runtime testing or shipped parity. GCP/Vertex is a separate named inventory delta. Domain lifecycle and professional review requirements complement the Codex mirror.

Team-only collaboration, necessary permitted evidence extracts, approved carry-forward and combined task/team budgets remain recommendations. Guest participation, evidence retention approach, cross-engagement recall, method distribution, reviewer hierarchy, cost limits, client/format/integration support, quantitative targets and release sequencing remain open. Broader portfolio/staffing/workflow-management capabilities are unprioritised additions, not replacements for V1 engagement assistance.

Pain reduction, demand and differentiation remain hypotheses. Success measures total effort through acceptance, including preparation, review, corrections, retries, delivery, cost and recovery; numerical targets require actual team baselines. Concept/branding exploration is not approved UX or demonstrated functionality. Native-record authority, professional approvals and processing policy must not be inferred from an editor or provider choice.
