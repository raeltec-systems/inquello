- 15.2/11.5: risk-based implementation confirmation, progress/tracking and missed-date discussion; management-reported completion, verified implementation, risk acceptance and escalation are distinct.

## Verification and readiness obligations

Faithful execution and professional judgment are distinct: establish what ran against the selected scope/population, with completed/excluded/failed/unfinished work and safe recovery without silent omission or duplicate effects. Spot checks or a finished model turn do not establish coverage. Exact architecture, benchmark, dataset, scale threshold and proof protocol are not selected by this extract.

Before real engagement use: demonstrate supported execution/recovery; verify access and data handling; expose work/evidence/failures/human review; agree authorised data/use with the organisation. During build: define supported procedures/sources/connections, recurrence and onboarding bounds, baseline, costs and export/link behaviour before reliance. After learning: broaden support/refine pricing without deferring the approved full journey. Establish costs/commercial arrangement before purchase. No launch date or quantified savings.
