## Qualitative experience, voice and feel

The brief asks for a practitioner-directed working experience: familiar existing artifacts, visible and inspectable agent work, adaptable methods, continuity across tools, and retained professional control. These qualities survive in PRD §1, the source journeys, FR-7/FR-8, FR-13, FR-19/FR-20, FR-43/FR-44 and NFR-5. The PRD does not reduce the experience to compliance gates or a fixed audit checklist.

The brief prescribes no specific conversational personality, visual style, colour system, layout or emotional brand promise. Those should not be invented as missing requirements. Existing brand concepts remain explicitly exploratory.
