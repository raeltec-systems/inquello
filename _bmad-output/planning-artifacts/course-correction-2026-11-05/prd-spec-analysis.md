Date: 2026-11-05. Status: proposed change analysis, not adopted requirements or implementation permission.
Scope: existing PRD/SPEC compared with approved PRFAQ and completed five-report research. Existing documents, code, installs, commits and Epic 1 are unchanged.

## Result and precedence
The material change is a connected internal-audit V1 journey, with risk-coverage planning promoted into scope and external-audit-specific work explicitly outside V1. It is not merely a smaller spreadsheet demonstration. The existing Codex-derived foundation and compatible generic capabilities remain valuable; the PRFAQ does not authorise deleting them or automatically changing every old phase label. A focused end-to-end release and full-parity timing require an explicit disposition ledger.
The approved journey does not choose native record authority, a database, or a mandatory workflow engine. Preserve the currently adopted external-record V1/native-authority V2 boundary until an explicit new decision. Reconcile how every journey stage obtains durable, connected working artifacts and designated authoritative records without using that boundary to weaken the journey.
Primary decision source: ../prfaq-inquello.md, Internal FAQ 6 and 9; supporting approved framing in Stage 1, Customer FAQ 1–10 and Internal FAQ 1/3/4/8. Detail pack: ../prfaq-inquello-distillate.md, Status and precedence; Approved V1 scope; Approved readiness sequence.

### P6 — Faithful execution and recovery (approved challenge; proof design still proposed)

## Concrete generic-capability examples for owner scope discussion
Line numbers below are one-based in the unchanged current PRD (499 lines), not this analysis. These illustrate breadth beyond the minimal three-cycle journey; they do not mean the capabilities lack value or should be removed.
1. **Voice/dictation and companions — §4.16 FR-31, lines 293–296.** Exact requirement: “Users can use supported voice/dictation, notifications and optional task companions.” PRFAQ does not explicitly exclude these; it does not make voice or companions necessary to its approved conversational journey. Current planning: Stories 11.5 (dictation), 11.6 (notification/companion).
2. **Additional access surfaces — §4.2, lines 93–105, FR-3/4.** Exact scope: “The target spans desktop, CLI/session controls, application-adjacent assistance, mobile/remote steering and optional physical controls.” FR-4: “Users retain applicable CLI/session commands, contextual application assistance and physical shortcuts from the inventory.” PRFAQ does not explicitly exclude these or replace OQ-6 desktop-first. Do not paraphrase this PRD text as a mandatory IDE client: it says application-adjacent assistance. Current planning: 11.1 CLI, 11.2 app-adjacent, 11.3 mobile/remote, 11.4 physical controls.
3. **Full provider-route breadth — §4.15 FR-29, lines 279–282.** Exact requirement: “Teams can use allowed models, organisational gateways, local-model routes and service identities, retaining Bedrock and Azure Foundry/Azure OpenAI routes and the named GCP/Vertex delta.” PRFAQ does not select a provider subset or rescind OQ-9. Particular pilot connections and model choices do not prove all these routes. Current planning: 9.2–9.10 future provider breadth; current initial routes are separately in 1.22/1.25.
4. **Programmatic/SDK and business-event breadth — §4.12 FR-23/24, lines 233–245.** Exact FR-23: “Teams can initiate permitted batch/SDK and non-interactive tasks and receive structured results and structured streaming where supported.” Exact FR-24: “Teams can configure supported schedules/events with trigger, inputs, Method, acting identity, budgets/limits, destination and escalation behaviour.” PRFAQ explicitly requires a supported recurring check; it leaves exact extent open and does not expressly exclude SDK, structured streams or business-event triggers. Therefore do not defer the recurring check merely because the wider automation epic was later. Current planning: 12.1 noninteractive/structured execution, 12.5 schedule/event mandate, 12.7 connected-service triggers.
5. **Application/site activity recall — §4.14 FR-28, lines 270–273.** Exact requirement: “Users can enable, inspect and control permitted recall of application/site activity separately from conversation history and reusable Methods.” PRFAQ requires connected sourced organisational context; that is not an explicit requirement for ambient application/site recall, and it does not explicitly prohibit it. 6. **Side chats/forks/alternatives — §4.13 FR-26, lines 256–259.** Exact requirement: “Users can create side chats, forks, references and alternate branches/analyses while retaining the relation to the originating Task.” PRFAQ's steer/question/revise experience does not explicitly require every branching surface or exclude it. Current planning examples: 9.1 client tools, 9.13 Code Mode; 2.15 hooks is explicitly backlog in the current tracker, and 1.21 current discovery is in-progress.
