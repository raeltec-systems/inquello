# Inquello glossary

Decided 2026-10-27. Overrides the PRD §3 **Assignment** entry; every other PRD §3 term stands. The final PRD, EXPERIENCE and Architecture Spine were updated to this vocabulary on 2026-10-27; older scope artifacts that still say Assignment are read as Engagement.

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | The broader working area for an audit team: its work, available resources, reusable procedures and settings. | "Acme Internal Audit" |
| **Engagement** | A particular audit or advisory engagement with objectives, scope, period, team members and outputs. | "Procurement Audit — 2026" |
| **Task** | A private-by-default conversation with Inquello directed towards an outcome, with its instructions, progress and resulting work; whole-Task sharing requires an explicit action. | "Analyse purchase orders for approval exceptions." |

One Workspace contains several Engagements (Procurement, Payroll, IT Access), each supported by multiple Tasks. A Task may be ad hoc, outside any Engagement (explain a policy, analyse a spreadsheet). The Engagement supplies shared audit context: objectives, scope, relevant evidence and related work. The Workspace supplies team context. Team-wide institutional memory spans different auditors and engagements of the same internal-audit company Workspace only for issued or explicitly team-shared work under current access. Private Tasks remain private; artifact sharing need not share a conversation. External-audit client/independence boundaries remain unresolved; see [collaboration and memory](collaboration-and-memory.md). In V1 this organisation sits alongside the team's external authoritative audit-record system.
