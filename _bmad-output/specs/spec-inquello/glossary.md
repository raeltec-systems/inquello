# Inquello glossary

Vocabulary decided 2026-10-27 and refined by the approved 2026-11-05 lifecycle. Engagement replaces the older Assignment term; current PRD §3 and the professional distinctions below govern. The final PRD, EXPERIENCE and Architecture Spine were updated to this vocabulary on 2026-10-27; older scope artifacts that still say Assignment are read as Engagement.

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | The broader working area for an audit team: its work, available resources, reusable procedures and settings. | "Acme Internal Audit" |
| **Engagement** | A particular audit or advisory engagement with objectives, scope, period, team members and outputs. | "Procurement Audit — 2026" |
| **Task** | A private-by-default conversation with Inquello directed towards an outcome, with its instructions, progress and resulting work; whole-Task sharing requires an explicit action. | "Analyse purchase orders for approval exceptions." |

One Workspace contains several Engagements (Procurement, Payroll, IT Access), each supported by multiple Tasks. A Task may be ad hoc, outside any Engagement (explain a policy, analyse a spreadsheet). The Engagement supplies shared audit context: objectives, scope, relevant evidence and related work. The Workspace supplies team context. Team-wide institutional memory spans different auditors and engagements of the same internal-audit company Workspace only for issued or explicitly team-shared work under current access. Private Tasks remain private; artifact sharing need not share a conversation. External-audit client/independence boundaries remain unresolved; see [collaboration and memory](collaboration-and-memory.md). In V1 these working relationships sit alongside designated external authoritative audit records, including file-based records; first validation selects no named external record system.


## Professional distinctions for connected work

| Term | Meaning and boundary |
|---|---|
| Audit universe | The organisation's auditable activities/entities/processes used to structure coverage planning; not a universal prescribed database schema. |
| Audit coverage plan | A sourced, risk-based proposal and its applicable human/governance decisions about what to audit; distinct from an individual engagement work programme. |
| Objective / scope / criterion | What the engagement seeks to achieve; its boundaries; and the benchmark against which evidence is evaluated. Preserve the distinctions as work changes. |
| Work programme | The planned procedures and related responsibilities/evidence needs under the chosen Method; changes retain their basis and applicable approval. |
| Exception / potential finding | A result requiring evaluation, not automatically a supported finding or repeated prior issue. |
| Finding / conclusion | An evaluated matter and the professional judgment reached from the work; evidence, significance and limitations stay inspectable. Root cause is pursued when possible, not presumed known. |
| Management action | Agreed response with responsible owner and due date; a reported completion does not prove implementation. |
| Implementation confirmation / risk acceptance | Evidence-based follow-up confirming action implementation versus an authorised decision to accept risk; neither is inferred from Task closure. |
