## Why and accepted breadth

Proposed WHAT framing: **Let audit and assurance teams bring existing work, direct scoped agent execution, inspect and revise evidence-backed outputs, and continue through accepted delivery in the tools and methods they use. Reduce total human effort while preserving accuracy, traceability, confidentiality and professional judgment.**

Support: PRD §1, lines 21–29; §7, lines 435–442. Codex-derived foundation, technical capability, extensibility, conversational continuity and retained existing files/tools are accepted direction. Internal/external audit, advisory and IT/IS across industries/methods remain in scope. One organisation's audit team is the first validation setting, not a market restriction. Ad hoc work must not require an Assignment. Demand, differentiation, savings and willingness to pay remain hypotheses.

Phase rule: PRD §1 line 27 and §6.1–6.2 lines 404–410: V1 is the full capability target excluding only FR-38/40/42. Scheduled/event work, approved unattended work and continuous assurance are V1. V2 adds native authoritative records, their lifecycle and deliberate authority migration. V2 foundations are not V1 gates. First shipping increment remains unselected; fixture slices do not redefine V1.

## Load-bearing constraints easy to lose during condensation

1. **Full mirror remains:** no narrowed MVP, implicit deferrals, parity claim from grouped requirements, or removal of smaller/technical capabilities; capability-specific acceptance applies to the whole inventory and each enabled route (PRD §0 L17; FR-18, FR-31–32, FR-44; §6 L404–410; addendum A16 L51).
2. **Distinct authorities:** tool/action permission, task instruction, reusable Method, evidence, model memory, agent review, human Professional Approval, Delivery and Record Authority are different. Agent titles establish neither approval nor professional independence (PRD vocabulary L60–71; FR-17/20/25/33–36; addendum §Method distinctions L57).
3. **Processing transparency:** local UI/execution does not establish local inference; files, execution, inference, connector/browser traffic, history/logs, backups and unattended runs require separate policy and route verification; no silent route substitution. Bedrock and Azure Foundry/Azure OpenAI remain explicit, and GCP/Vertex remains the named inventory delta, without provider approval (PRD L29, FR-5/29; NFR-8 L396; addendum L14–18).
4. **Component/service eligibility:** pinned Codex source and current docs may differ; core/CLI/app-server/TS SDK are candidate components, SDK wraps CLI and does not provide desktop UI; reusable desktop source and full hosted tool/connector/scheduler implementations were not established. Each capability needs a supported route plus operational validation (addendum L14–16). Adopt later architecture choices without converting this evidence limitation into a false reuse claim.
5. **Artifact identity and fidelity:** keep same supported saved files editable externally; preserve unrelated content and supported formulas/cache, references, comments/revisions, charts/themes/notes; warn before destructive unsupported conversions; detect concurrent human edits (FR-6/13; NFR-1/9). Saved files, snapshots and supported live/unsaved context are separate claims (vocabulary L66; FR-4/10; addendum L63).
6. **Actual coverage/provenance:** retained input scope/version, Method/version/parameters, derivations, exclusions, duplicate handling, uncertainty, applied effects and human decisions must support review; changed evidence/Methods/content identify affected outputs/approvals. Successful runs and Task/token counts cannot establish complete Coverage (FR-9/11/12/20/37/43–44; NFR-2/7/10).
7. **Delivery safeguards:** inspect destination, identity, proposed fields and evidence; preserve unrelated fields; check fresh version; declare missing conditional-update/deduplication support; reconcile uncertain results before retry. Transport success is not work success or confirmed Delivery. Audit-record writeback grants no operational transaction-editing right (FR-41; PRD §8 L448; addendum L18/28).
8. **Cancellation is not rollback:** cancellation/resumption/history modification does not undo applied file/remote effects; preserve usable retained work and report applied effects, loss and recovery limits (FR-8; NFR-4; addendum L18/A09).
9. **Unattended accountability:** declared trigger, scope, inputs, Method, identity, budget, destination and escalation; distinguish missing event, unrun, incomplete, failed and no-exception results; duplicate control and revocation/reassignment cover ongoing jobs (FR-24/36; NFR-3/4/10).
10. **Trust boundaries:** retrieved instructions are untrusted source data; methods do not grant technical permission; hook configuration/success alone does not prove complete enforcement or attack detection (FR-15/22/33–34; addendum M1 L30 and L57).
11. **Memory and reuse:** memory use and contribution controls remain separate; personal preferences, approved reusable knowledge and Assignment/client evidence remain distinct; cross-client/Assignment recall is not assumed authorised; case correction must not silently become a team rule (FR-19/20/27/28; addendum M1).
12. **Human responsibility:** responsibilities may overlap where policy permits; no fixed firm hierarchy. Review identifies authorised person, scope/version; current access revocation and retained data handling are distinct; orphaned jobs need policy handling (PRD §2.1 L35–43; FR-35/36/40).
13. **Practice flexibility:** no mandatory engagement, control object, deficiency status, universal sampling rule or common workflow state machine; external-audit assertions/materiality/substantive work, advisory options and actual technical/security work remain first-class (UJ-1/5/6; FR-12/18/39/44; addendum A02/A06).
14. **Policy values remain gates:** initial format/client/record contracts, retention/recall, provider eligibility, budgets, latency/scale/recovery/accessibility targets, named humans and shipping order are not invented defaults; later adopted documents may resolve only their explicitly decided portions (PRD §§8/10/11).
15. **Truthful evidence status:** all W1–W3/M1 and A01–A18 scenarios are proposed/unexecuted; intended metrics are not measured benefits. Illustrative personas are not observed practitioners; proposed role owners are not named appointments (PRD L47, L427, L464, L483–486; addendum L10).
